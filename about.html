About Paradise Taste

Paradise Taste opened in 2024 to give people in Freetown a clean and welcoming place to enjoy delicious home-style cooking at affordable prices. We prepare our meals using fresh ingredients from local markets and farmers. Our goal is to provide good food, excellent customer service and a comfortable environment where families and friends can enjoy their meals.

What We Stand For

Freshness

We prepare our meals fresh every day using locally sourced vegetables, meat, fish and traditional Sierra Leonean spices.

Fair Prices

We believe everyone deserves quality food at affordable prices. Our menu clearly displays the prices of our meals.

Hygiene

Cleanliness is very important at Paradise Taste. Our staff follow food safety practices to maintain a clean kitchen and dining environment.

Local Sourcing

We support local farmers and businesses by purchasing fresh vegetables, fish, rice and other ingredients from markets in Freetown.
